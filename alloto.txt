User Manual

1. Getting Started
- Open the invoice application.
- Log in with your credentials.
- Navigate to the main dashboard.

2. Creating an Invoice
- Click 'New Invoice'.
- Enter customer details and items.
- Review totals and save the invoice.

3. Managing Invoices
- View existing invoices from the dashboard.
- Edit or delete invoices as needed.
- Export invoices for records.

4. Support
- Check the help section for FAQs.
- Contact support if you need assistance.
